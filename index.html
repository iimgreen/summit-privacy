SUMMIT Watch Face

Privacy Policy · Last updated November 4, 2026 · Developer: iiMBlue

The short version: SUMMIT collects nothing, and nothing leaves your watch.

SUMMIT is a watch face for Garmin watches. This policy explains what data it uses and what happens to it.

What the watch face reads

To draw the face, SUMMIT reads data your Garmin watch already holds:

  • Time, date and the watch's display settings (12/24-hour, units)
  • Activity data: steps and step goal, floors climbed, calories
  • Health data the watch records: heart rate, Body Battery, stress
  • Altitude
  • Battery level
  • Weather conditions provided by Garmin to the watch
  • Status: Bluetooth connection, notification count, alarms, Do Not Disturb

What happens to that data

  • It is used only on the watch, only to display it on the face.
  • It is not stored by SUMMIT, apart from your own face settings (colorway, time style, and which data each slot shows), which stay on the watch and in your Garmin Connect settings.
  • It is never sent anywhere: SUMMIT makes no network connections and has no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • It is not shared with or sold to anyone.

Permissions

SUMMIT asks the watch for two permissions:

  • Sensor history: to show Body Battery, stress and altitude.
  • Complication subscriber: to read the data choices you make in the watch's own face editor.

Neither permission sends data off the watch.

Children

SUMMIT does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
